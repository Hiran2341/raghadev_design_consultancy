Every build follows the same rigorous 15-step process below — the difference is in how we tailor materials, structure, and design to your specific use case.

New homes, villas, individual houses, extensions, and complete renovations. Built around how your family lives.

Commercial

Offices Retail Spaces Mixed-Use Complexes
Phase 1
Groundwork & Foundation
1
🧪
Soil & Water Testing
Before anything is built, we test the soil load-bearing capacity and check groundwater levels. This determines the type of foundation your building needs — whether a simple strip foundation, raft slab, or deep pile foundation for soft or waterlogged ground.
Soil Test Report Water Table Analysis Load Bearing Study
2
🏗️
Material Selection — Steel, Cement & Brick
Based on soil results and structural load, we select the right grade of materials. TMT steel grade, cement type (OPC 43 vs 53), and brick type (solid, hollow, or AAC blocks) are chosen for your specific climate, soil condition, and building weight.
Fe500 TMT Steel OPC 53 / PPC Cement AAC / Solid Brick
3
⬛
Raising the Foundation
Excavation, PCC (Plain Cement Concrete) bedding, and foundation construction begins. The basement or plinth level is raised to the correct height above ground, protecting the structure from moisture ingress, flooding, and settlement over time.
PCC Bedding RCC Foundation Anti-Termite Treatment Waterproofing
Phase 2
Structural Frame
4
🧱
Selecting Concrete Grade
Concrete grade is selected based on your location and exposure conditions — coastal areas (like Puducherry) demand sulphate-resistant cement and higher grade M25+ concrete to resist seawater salinity. Inland builds may use M20. We design the mix precisely for your environment.
M20 / M25 / M30 Sulphate-Resistant Coastal Mix Design
5
⚡
Electrical Layout in Roof Slab
Before the roof slab is cast, all electrical conduit pipes, switch board positions, and lighting point boxes are laid into the formwork. This is critical — routing electrical lines through a finished slab is impossible. Every point is planned on paper first, then laid precisely before pouring.
ISI Conduit Pipes Switch Board Layout Fan & Light Points AC & Exhaust Points
6
🏛️
Completing Skeleton & Brickwork
Columns, beams, and slabs form the structural skeleton. Once the skeleton is complete, brickwork begins — filling the walls between columns. Courses are laid plumb and level, with proper bonding pattern, and mortar joints are struck neatly for a solid, gap-free wall.
RCC Columns & Beams Solid / Hollow Bricks Lintel Bands
Phase 3
Electrical & Plumbing
7
🔨🛠️🔧
Laying Electrical & Plumbing Lines
Chasing (cutting channels in walls) for conduit pipes and plumbing lines happens before plastering. Hot and cold water supply lines, drainage runs, and electrical circuits are routed through the walls. Every layout is drawn up and checked before cutting begins to avoid structural damage.
Wall Chasing CPVC / UPVC Pipes Circuit Planning Earth Bonding
Phase 4
Interior Finishing
8
🪣🪏
Plastering the Walls
Two coats: a rough backing coat, followed by a smooth finishing coat . All corners are straightened with angle beads. Ceiling plastering is done before walls. Curing is done for a minimum of 7 days to prevent cracking. The quality of plastering directly affects how paint or putty looks later.
Backing Coat 12mm Finish Coat 6mm 7-Day Curing Corner Beads
9
🧴
Selecting & Applying Putty
Wall putty fills micro-pores and hairline cracks left after plastering, creating a perfectly smooth base for paint. We help you choose between cement-based putty (durable, external use) and polymer putty (smoother finish, internal walls). Two coats are applied, with sanding between each.
Birla White / Asian Putty 2 Coats 120 Grit Sanding
10
🔲💎⬜️
Selecting Flooring Type
Flooring is chosen for each room based on use, aesthetics, and maintenance. Vitrified tiles for living areas, anti-skid ceramic for bathrooms and kitchens, granite or marble for premium spaces, and outdoor-grade tiles for terraces. We advise on size, pattern, and grout colour too.
Vitrified Tiles Ceramic / Anti-Skid Granite / Marble Outdoor Porcelain
11
🎨🖌️
Selecting Paint Type
Not all paints are the same. Interior walls use emulsion (matt or silk sheen), wet areas use moisture-resistant paint, and exterior walls need weather-shield paint that handles heavy rain, heat, and UV exposure — especially important in the Puducherry climate. We help you pick sheen level, brand, and colour palette.
Interior Emulsion Exterior Weather-Shield Moisture-Resistant Texture / Primer
Phase 5
Fit-Out & Fixtures
12
🔌💡🎛️
Electrical Wires, Switches & Boards
Final electrical wiring is pulled through the conduits laid earlier. Wire gauge is selected based on load — 1.5 sq mm for lighting, 4 sq mm for power sockets, 6 sq mm for AC and heavy appliances. Switch plates, MCB boards, and modular switches are fitted. All circuits are tested before final connection.
Finolex / Havells Wire MCB Distribution Board Modular Switches Earth Leakage Protection
13
🚿🚽🔧
Selecting Fixtures & Fittings
Sanitary fittings, bathroom fixtures, kitchen sink, taps, shower heads, and toilet units are selected and fitted. We advise on brand, finish (chrome, matte, brushed gold), water-saving ratings, and durability to balance aesthetics with performance and long-term maintenance.
Jaquar / Kohler / Cera WELS Water Rating Chrome / Matte Black Anti-Limescale
Phase 6
Handover
14
🏠
Finalising the Building
Door frames, architraves, window shutters, grille work, external cladding, and compound wall finishing are all completed. False ceiling (if applicable), cabinetry, and exterior landscaping happens at this stage too. A complete punch-list is generated and all items signed off before declaring the structure finished.
Door & Window Frames False Ceiling Exterior Cladding Punch-List Signoff
15
✨
Cleaning & Final Handover
A professional deep-clean removes construction dust, cement smears, and debris from every surface. All tiles are cleaned and polished, glass is cleared, fixtures are tested, and electrical systems are final-checked. We walk through the property with you room by room, explain every system, and hand over keys only when everything meets our standard — and yours.
Deep Clean Systems Walkthrough Warranty Documents Key Handover
