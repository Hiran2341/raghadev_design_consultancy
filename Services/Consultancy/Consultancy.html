Your First Conversation

"Every great project starts with a conversation — not a contract. We listen first, then advise."

Whether you have a clear plan or just an idea on a napkin, our initial consultation is a relaxed, no-obligation discussion where we understand your goals, your budget expectations, and the scope of what you want to achieve.

On the Ground

Site Visit & Assessment

After the first consultation, we visit your site to see the land, structure, or space firsthand. This lets us give you accurate, grounded advice — not estimates made from a desk.

📐
Site Measurements
We record accurate dimensions of the plot, existing structures, and surroundings.
🔍
Condition Check
We assess existing structures for stability, drainage, access, and potential issues.
📍
Location Analysis
We review local planning context, neighbouring properties, and site constraints.
📋
Site Report
You receive a written summary of findings and our recommended approach.

Full Scope of Support

Everything We Cover for You

Our consultancy service isn't just one meeting — it's ongoing expert guidance covering every aspect of your project from idea to approval. Here's what we help you with.

01
Feasibility Study
We assess whether your project is viable — structurally, financially, and from a planning perspective — before you commit a single rupee.
02
Planning Guidance
We advise on local planning rules, what permissions you need, and the best approach to get approvals smoothly.
03
Budget Roadmap
We provide a realistic cost framework so you can plan finances confidently from the start — no surprises later.
04
Design Direction
We help shape your design concept — advising on style, materials, and spatial planning before drawings begin.
05
Contractor Coordination
We help you understand how to select and brief the right contractors, subcontractors, and specialists for your project.
06
Ongoing Advisory
Questions come up throughout a project. We remain available throughout to give you answers, resolve issues, and keep things moving.
